## 0.10.0: Bring your own NPC

Two big things: NPCs can now wear a model you made yourself, and the mail system is in.

### Custom NPC models
Build a model in Blockbench, animate it, and put it in the game. No JSON, no resource pack to
assemble by hand.

Export it as a **GeckoLib Animated Model**, drop the files in the import folder, then in game go to
an NPC's Look tab, Choose Model, Manage models, New model. Pick the files from the lists, give it a
name, press Create. It appears in the model picker with the built-in ones, and NPCs wearing it walk,
idle and stand about with your animations.

Every field is a picker over something that exists. The model, texture and animation lists come from
the import folder, and the clip lists come from reading the animation file you just chose, so there
is never a name to type that has to match exactly. Clips called idle and walk are found on their own.

The **Import folder** button opens the folder in your desktop's file browser, so getting exports
there is drag and drop.

If something is wrong it says so in plain words while you are still looking at the screen:

- "That is not a GeckoLib model. In Blockbench use File, Export, GeckoLib Animated Model."
- "This model expects a 64 by 64 texture, but that image is 128 by 128."

That second one is worth the trouble. A mismatched texture is the most common reason a model looks
scrambled in game, and nothing otherwise tells you.

**Manage models** lists what is installed with its author and clip count, and a Remove on each. An
NPC still wearing a removed model quietly goes back to the built-in one.

For now the model has to be on each player's own game. Sharing them from a server is next.

### NPCs that actually move
NPCs on the built-in GeckoLib model stood in one idle loop whether they were rooted to a spot or
walking a patrol route. They now walk while they walk, and the Statue, Breathe and Lively setting
means the same thing on that model as it already did on the vanilla ones. Lively adds occasional
flourishes, worked out per NPC so two standing side by side do not move in lockstep.

A resource pack can add clips to the built-in model too, and a Clip row on the Pose tab lists
whatever GeckoLib has actually loaded.

### Mail
A mailbox block. Place one, use it, and it is yours. It puts its flag up when something is waiting,
and sits on the floor or on a wall depending where you put it.

Mail arrives as a **parcel item**. It holds up to six stacks plus coins, says who sent it and what is
inside on its tooltip, and can be carried off, dropped in a chest, given to somebody or opened
whenever you like. Right click to open it.

- **Inbox** is a grid of parcels. Drag one out, or press Take all to open the lot at once.
- **Outbox** posts one. Pick who from a list of everyone with a mailbox, fill the slots, add coins
  and a note, send.
- Auction winnings arrive as a parcel from the Auction House, so there is one place to look.
- If the person you picked is online, a **Trade** button starts a live trade with them.

The trading post folded into this block rather than being a second one to place.

### Smaller
- **Screens fit small windows.** The mod's screens step their scale down rather than running off the
  edge of the display.
- **A talk bubble** can be turned on for an NPC worth stopping for, floating above its head.
- Typing in the mailbox note field no longer closes the window.
