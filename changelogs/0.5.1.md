## 0.5.1: Tester-feedback patch

A round of fixes on top of 0.5.0, straight from tester feedback.

### Fixes
- **NPCs pause when you talk to them**: they stop wandering and turn to face you while you're
  interacting, then carry on once you're done.
- **Statue holds under animation packs**: a Statue-posed NPC now stays frozen even with
  Fresh Animations / Fresh Moves installed. Every other pose still gets the pack's animations, so your
  NPCs keep their life.
- **Waystone fees on hover**: hovering a destination in the waystone menu shows its teleport fee, with
  the dimensional fee on cross-dimension trips. This also fixes the fee not showing at all on 1.21.
- **Smoother NPC model picker**: model previews load as you scroll to them instead of all at once, so
  the picker no longer lags on large modpacks.
