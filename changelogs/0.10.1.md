## 0.10.1

0.10.0 was tagged but never published, so everything in it is here too. If you are coming from
0.9.0, this is the lot.

### Shops keep their contents when an NPC goes missing

Reported from a live server: NPCs vanishing "along with the money and inventory in them".

The money was never deleted. A shop and everything in it is saved separately from the NPC that runs
it, so losing the NPC leaves the shop intact with nobody standing behind it. What was missing was
any way back to it. Placing a replacement NPC built a second, empty shop and left the first one
holding the owner's stock and takings, which from the owner's side looks exactly like theft.

- Placing a shop NPC now tells you when you already own a shop with no shopkeeper, and prints the
  command
- `/shop relink <id>` puts the NPC you are looking at behind one of your shops. You need to own
  both, and it refuses if that NPC already runs something

It is offered rather than done automatically. From the server's side an NPC standing in an unloaded
chunk is indistinguishable from a deleted one, so adopting on its own would occasionally take a shop
off a shopkeeper that was merely out of range.

`/shop admin cleanup` was the other half of that problem. It decided an NPC no longer existed by
failing to find it, and looked only in the dimension the operator was standing in. Run over a market
district nobody had walked through, it would cut every shop loose from its shopkeeper in one command
with no way back. It now searches every dimension and only reports; acting needs
`/shop admin cleanup confirm`.

An NPC also re-asserts its never-remove flag every time it loads, so worlds carried forward from
versions that did not set it repair themselves.

### Heart crystals

The rarest thing in the mod. Eat one for a permanent extra heart, up to two extra rows: twenty
crystals, ten hearts to thirty.

Dying takes one back, down to none, never touching the ten you start with. `/hearts loseondeath
false` turns that off for a server. `/hearts` tells you where you stand.

The count lives in the world save rather than on the player, so a death cannot quietly refund or
delete one depending on what keepInventory is set to.

| Where | Chance per open |
|---|---|
| Common Crate | 0.03% |
| Rare Crate | 0.09% |
| Epic Crate | 0.2% |
| Balloon | 0.1% |
| Golden Cache | 0.3% |

Admin NPCs can sell them: their stock is any item stack.

### Mail

A mailbox block. Place one and it is yours; the flag goes up when something is waiting, and it sits
on the floor or mounts on a wall. Break it and the mail is still there when you put a new one down.

Mail arrives as a **parcel item**, not a row in a screen. Up to six stacks plus coins, with the
sender and contents on the tooltip. Carry it, chest it, give it away, open it next week.

- **Inbox**: a grid of parcels. Drag one out, or Take all
- **Outbox**: pick a recipient, fill the slots, add coins and a note
- Auction winnings arrive as a parcel, so there is one place to look
- If your recipient is online, a **Trade** button opens a live trade instead

### Bring your own NPC model

Model and animate in Blockbench, export as a **GeckoLib Animated Model**, drop the files in the
import folder, and build it in the editor: Look, Choose Model, Manage models, New model.

Every field is a picker over something that exists, so there is no name to type that has to match.
Clip lists are read out of the animation file you chose, and `idle` and `walk` are found on their
own. Mistakes are explained on the spot, including the expensive one: *"This model expects a 64 by
64 texture, but that image is 128 by 128."*

**Sharing**: an operator presses Share and the model goes to the server, kept in the world folder,
so copying the world copies the models. Players get it on their next join, and a player who already
has it downloads nothing. Nothing is pushed at anybody mid-session.

### NPCs

- NPCs on the built-in model walk while they walk, instead of standing in one idle loop
- **Daily schedules**: send an NPC somewhere at a time of day, walking its own route to get there
- An NPC with **gravity turned off** can now move. Vanilla's ground navigation refuses to build a
  path unless the mob is standing on something, so a floating NPC could not wander, patrol or follow
  anybody. Floating NPCs now use the navigation vanilla gives bees and allays

### World

- **Golden Caches** generate as the world is explored: buried under an oak, in about one chunk in
  three thousand, worked out from the seed so a chunk always gives the same answer. Only one may sit
  unopened at a time, so covering ground quickly is not a way to farm them. Both configurable
- **Balloon crates** wait to be told where. Until an area is set nothing spawns and nothing is
  announced, instead of announcing a wave that never arrived
- Optionally every online player gets their own balloon on the same wave, measured from their own
  height. Underground with no room above you, you get none
- The balloon settings in the config screen now reach the world

### Smaller

- The mailbox has a real model, and its flag has an up and a down
- Mailboxes on walls need the wall, and drop when it goes
