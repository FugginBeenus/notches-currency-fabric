## 0.11.0

### Crates got a makeover
New crate models with an open animation, one texture per tier. Thanks to @FFlash
for the art. The inventory icon is the crate itself now instead of a drawn
sprite, and it shows up that way in your hand, in item frames and on the ground.

Sounds follow the animation instead of firing all at once on the click: a heavy
wooden thud as it starts moving, the lid swinging up a second later with your
reward, and a thud as it shuts.

### Fixed worlds hanging on load
On 1.21.11 worlds would sit on the loading screen and never finish, and could
not be saved. Golden caches were looking for a spot the instant a chunk loaded,
which left the server waiting on itself.

### Fixed 1.21.11 resource loading
The mod shipped a pack.mcmeta that 1.21.11 reads as both a resource pack and a
data pack, and those two want opposite formats, so there was no version of the
file that worked. Fabric fills it in correctly on its own, so it is gone. The
NPC models pack also stopped rebuilding itself on every world join.
