## 0.5.3 — Shop purchase fix

### Fixes
- **Shops now sell the whole stack the listing shows.** If you listed 32 sculk sensors for 15 coins,
  buyers were charged the full 15 and handed a single sensor, and only one came off the stock. Buying
  now gives the whole stack for the listed price. Both Minecraft versions were affected; listings that
  sell single items behave exactly as before, and existing listings are fixed as soon as you update —
  nothing to re-create.
- Removing a listing with more than a stack of items in it now returns all of them. It used to stop at
  64 and the rest was lost.
- Shift-clicking a listing buys several whole stacks of it, instead of loose items.

### Performance
- Crowds of NPCs are much lighter to render. The skin's outer layer is dropped past 20 blocks and
  floating names past 32, so a town full of NPCs costs closer to what ordinary mobs do.

Thanks to the server admins who reported the shop bug with a clear repro — that one was worth catching.
