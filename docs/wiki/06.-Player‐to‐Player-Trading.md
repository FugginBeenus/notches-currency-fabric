## Player-to-Player Trading

Two ways to trade directly with other players: **live trades** (both online) and **offline trade
offers** (accept any time).

### Live Trading

A secure, face-to-face trade where both players are online.

1. **`/trade <player>`** sends an invite.
2. The other player accepts with **`/trade accept <name>`** (or declines with `/trade decline`).
3. A trade window opens. Each side places items into their offer; you see the other side mirror in
   real time.
4. Both parties must **confirm** before the trade executes - changing your offer un-confirms it, so
   nobody can swap items at the last second.

### Offline Trade Offers

Post a standing offer that someone can accept later, even if you've logged off.

1. **`/trade offer`** opens the create screen.
2. Drop the item you're **giving** into the top slot (it's held in escrow).
3. Set a **price**: a coin amount and/or a *sample* of an item you want in return.
4. Optionally name a **target player** (leave blank to let anyone accept).
5. **Create Offer.**

Anyone eligible can then run **`/trade offers`** to see offers directed at them, and **Accept**. The
same screen lists **your** open offers with a **Cancel** button (which returns your escrowed items).

**Offline-safe delivery:** when an offer is accepted, coins go to the creator's balance and any items
they were paid go to a **mailbox** - delivered automatically the next time they log in. You can have
up to **10** open offers at once.

### Commands

| Command | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `/trade <player>` | Invite a player to a live trade |
| `/trade accept <name>` | Accept a live-trade invite |
| `/trade decline <name>` | Decline a live-trade invite |
| `/trade offer` | Create an offline trade offer |
| `/trade offers` | Browse offers for you + manage your own |
