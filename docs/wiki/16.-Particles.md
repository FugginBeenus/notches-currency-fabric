## Particles

Give an NPC a look. A wizard trailing motes, a ghost bleeding wisps, a blacksmith throwing sparks.
You build an **effect** once, give it a name, and any NPC can wear it or fire it.

Open an NPC, go to **Manage**, and press **Particles**.

### Two kinds of effect, one recipe

- **Standing.** The NPC wears it and it runs all the time. This is the main use: what an NPC *is*,
  seen from across a street.
- **Triggered.** It fires once from a **Play particles** action, so any reaction, dialogue choice or
  quest can use it. No extra setup.

Both come out of the same editor.

### The library

The Particles screen lists every effect in the world. Name one in the box and press **+ New effect**
to build it, click a row to edit it, or the red **x** to delete it. Effects are saved with the
world, up to 64 of them.

At the bottom, **This NPC wears:** shows which effect the NPC you came from is wearing. Click it to
cycle through your effects.

A fresh world starts with six: **wizard**, **ghost**, **blacksmith**, **holy**, **cursed** and
**hearth**. They are meant to be pulled apart and learned from. On a world made before this
version, run `/npcfx starters` to add them.

### The editor

Four tabs on the left, a live preview on the right. The preview shows the NPC with dots where each
particle will appear, and it moves as you change things.

**Look**

- **Particle** - type a name, or press **Pick** to browse (see below).
- **Colour** - for the dusts and for all of our own particles. **Auto** picks a sensible default.
  **Rainbow** gives each particle its own hue and slowly cycles them.
- **Fade to** - the second colour, for fading dust only.
- **Frame hold** - how many ticks a glyph or animation frame stays before it changes.

**Shape**

- **Draw as** - **Solid shape** redraws the whole shape every tick so it holds together and can
  move as one thing. **Spray** lets each particle be born on the shape and drift off on its own,
  for smoke and sparks. Solid is the default.
- **Shape** - Point, Ring, Ball, Cone, Column or Spiral.
- **Size** - the radius or height, in blocks.
- **Anchor** - feet, chest, head, left hand or right hand. Hands turn with the NPC.
- **Nudge X, Y, Z** - move the layer around. Shift steps by a quarter block.

**Move**

- **Motion** - **Still**, **Spin** (the shape turns around the NPC) or **Rise** (the shape climbs
  and wraps). A ring that sits there is a puddle. A ring that turns is a spell.
- **Motion speed** - how fast it turns or climbs.

**Spray** (only matters when Draw as is Spray)

- **Count** - how many come out at once.
- **Life** - how many ticks each particle lives. **1** is a single crisp frame. **Natural** leaves
  the particle to its own timing.
- **Rate**, **Speed**, **Spread** - how often, how fast they fly off, how loosely they scatter.

Two different speeds, and it is worth being blunt: **Speed** moves the particles. **Motion speed**
moves the shape they come out of.

### Layers

An effect holds up to **four layers**, each with its own particle, shape and settings. The strip on
the left switches between them. **+ add** makes a new one, **Drop** removes the selected one. A
wizard is a ring of glyphs on one layer and a spark at the hand on another.

### Test, Wear, Hide

- **Test** fires the effect once on the NPC you came from.
- **Wear** puts the effect on that NPC for good. The button turns gold and reads **Live**, and the
  real particles run behind the editor while you work. Press **Save** and the NPC picks up your
  change within a second. Press **Live** again to take it off.
- **Hide** tucks the editor away so you can look at the NPC, with one **Show editor** button to
  bring it back.

### Picking a particle

Press **Pick** next to the particle box. You get a list with plain names, sorted into groups:
**Notch**, **Fire**, **Magic**, **Nature**, **Water**, **Sky** and **Combat**. Search matches the
plain name or the real id. Hover a row and a little card shows the actual picture.

Some vanilla particles **ignore where you put them**. `enchant` flies toward a table and drops,
`note` reads its speed as a colour, `portal` goes its own way. They are marked **wanders** in the
list, and in the editor a red line offers a tame twin. Click it to swap.

### Our own particles

Seven particles that wear vanilla's pictures but run our code: they sit where they are put, take a
colour, and die exactly on time.

| Name | Looks like |
| --- | --- |
| `notchcurrency:glyph` | the galactic letters, without the dive |
| `notchcurrency:portal` | the purple swirl, without flying off |
| `notchcurrency:note` | the music note, in any colour |
| `notchcurrency:spark` | the sparkle, without the drift |
| `notchcurrency:spell` | the spell puff |
| `notchcurrency:glow` | a soft glow |
| `notchcurrency:dot` | a plain soft dot |

### Your own pictures

Press **Textures...** on the Particles screen. There are **eight slots**.

1. Press **Open folder** and drop a PNG in.
2. Click a slot, type a name, and press **Import into** that slot.
3. It shows up in the picker under **Notch** by that name, with a thumbnail.

A picture should be square, **2 to 64 pixels** wide, and under 256 KB. 8 or 16 is normal. For an
animation, stack the frames top to bottom in one tall picture. It plays at 10 frames a second, and
**Frame hold** in the editor slows it down.

Shared with the server the same way sounds are: an operator's import goes up at once, and other
players get it on their next join.

### Keeping servers happy

Three guards run without you touching anything: nothing is calculated unless a player is within
32 blocks, a solid shape stops at 48 particles, and each player's game spawns no more than 600
particles a tick across every NPC it can see. A town full of wizards costs nothing to a player on
the other side of the map.

### Commands

| Command | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `/npcfx starters` | add the six starter effects to a world that does not have them |
| `/npcfx try <particle> <shape> <count> <motion>` | fire a quick one-off on the nearest NPC, for testing |
