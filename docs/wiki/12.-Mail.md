## Mail

A mailbox block, and mail that arrives as an item you can pick up and carry rather than a list in a
screen.

### The mailbox

Place one and it is yours. It puts its flag up when something is waiting and puts it down when the
box is empty, so you can tell from across the street. It sits on the floor or mounts on a wall
depending where you put it, and a wall one drops if the wall it is on goes away.

Breaking a mailbox does not lose mail. Put a new one down and everything that was waiting is still
there. It tells you so when you break one that had something in it.

### Parcels

Mail arrives as a **parcel item**. A parcel holds up to six stacks plus coins, and its tooltip says
who sent it and what is inside without opening it.

Because it is an item, it behaves like one. Carry it around, drop it in a chest, hand it to somebody
else, or leave it sealed and open it next week. Right click to open it.

### The two tabs

- **Inbox** - a grid of the parcels waiting for you. Drag one out to take it, or press **Take all**
  to unwrap the lot at once
- **Outbox** - post one. Pick who from a list of everyone with a mailbox, fill the slots, add coins
  and a note, and send

Auction winnings arrive as a parcel from the Auction House, so there is one place to look for
everything owed to you.

### Trading instead

If the person you picked in the Outbox is online, a **Trade** button appears and opens a live trade
with them instead. The trading post folded into this block rather than being a second thing to
place.

### Notes

- Nothing is handed to you on login. Mail is collected at a mailbox on purpose, and you are told on
  join if something is waiting
- An NPC with the **Mailbox** role opens the same screen, if you would rather have a postmaster than
  a block
