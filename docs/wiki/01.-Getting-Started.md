## Getting Started

### Earning Coins

Coins enter the economy through **faucets**:

- Popping **balloon crates** that spawn periodically
- Finding **golden caches** in the world
- Killing bosses (Ender Dragon, Wither, Elder Guardian)
- Completing **bounties** at a bounty board
- Selling items in the **auction house** or your **shop**
- Trading with other players

### Banking Your Coins

Physical **Notch Coins** are items you can lose on death. Deposit them into your safe **virtual
balance** at an **ATM / Bank** block:

1. Place or find an ATM.
2. Right-click to open it.
3. Drop coins into the deposit slot, or withdraw balance back into physical coins.

Your balance is shown on the **HUD** and can be checked any time with `/receipts` for a full history.

### Opening a Shop

Shops are run by NPCs. To open one:

1. Get a **Notch NPC** item (creative tab) and place it - you now own that NPC.
2. Sneak + right-click the NPC to open its **editor**, go to the **Role** tab, and choose **Shop**.
3. Right-click your NPC to open the **shop manager**, then add listings (see [Shops](04.-Shops)).

That's it - no license or spawn egg needed anymore. The single NPC item does everything.
