## Currency System

### Notch Coins

The currency exists in two forms:

| Form | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Physical Coins** | An item in your inventory. Can be dropped, stored, and traded - but lost on death. |
| **Virtual Balance** | Stored digitally per-player. Used for purchases and shown on the HUD. Safe. |

Convert between the two at an **ATM / Bank** block. Every change to your balance is validated
server-side and written to an audit log.

### The Balance HUD

Your current balance is always visible on the HUD (position is configurable). The counter animates
smoothly when your balance changes, and the coin symbol (`⛁`) appears in the HUD, in chat, and on the
coin item.

### Receipts

Run **`/receipts`** to open your transaction history: a paginated list of recent coin movements
showing the amount (green for earned, red for spent), what it was for, and how long ago. The most
recent 50 transactions are kept.

### Sending Coins

- **`/pay <player> <amount>`** - send coins directly to another player. A payment of 10,000 or
  more asks you to confirm first: click **[Confirm]** or run the same command again within 30
  seconds. The threshold is **Confirm /pay above** in the config; 0 turns it off.

### Starting Balance

New players start with nothing. Set **Starting balance** in the config to hand every brand-new
player a few coins the first time they join. It is a faucet, so keep it small. Off by default.

### Custom Currency

You can give the coin a **custom name and artwork** that applies everywhere - the item, HUD, chat
symbol, and every message and GUI. On a server it's pushed to every player automatically. See the
dedicated **[Currency Creator](03.-Currency-Creator)** page for the full rundown.

### Recipes

**ATM / Bank**

```
I I I
I E I
I G I

I = Iron Ingot   E = Emerald   G = Gold Block
```

Most other blocks and items (NPC item, crate keys, gambling and info blocks) are available in the
**Notch Currency** creative tab.
