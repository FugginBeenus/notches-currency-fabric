## Shops

Player shops let anyone sell items for coins, bartered items, or both. Every shop is run by a
**Notch NPC** with the **Shop** role.

> **Note:** The old Merchant License and Shopkeeper Spawn Egg have been retired. The single Notch NPC
> item now handles everything.

### Creating a Shop

1. Place a **Notch NPC** item (creative tab). You own the NPC that appears.
2. **Sneak + right-click** the NPC to open its editor.
3. On the **Role** tab, choose **Shop**. A shop is created and linked to that NPC.

### Managing Your Shop

**Right-click** your own shop NPC to open the **Shop Manager**:

- **Earnings** - coins from sales collect here; click **Collect** to move them (and any bartered
  items) to your balance/inventory.
- **Name & Greeting** - set the shop's display name and the greeting shoppers see.
- **Open / Closed** - toggle whether the shop is currently selling.
- **Listings** - every listing is shown, paginated (up to **27**). Each has an **Edit** button, and
  there's a **+ New Listing** button.

### Adding & Editing Listings

Open a listing (or **+ New Listing**) to reach the **listing editor**:

- **Sale item** - drop a *sample* of the item into the top slot. Its count is the whole bundle one
  trade moves, so a sample of 5 coral means every trade is 5 coral at once. Samples are never
  consumed and come back when you close.
- **Price** - what a shopper pays you. Set coins, and/or drop a *sample* into the barter slot (its
  count is how many are needed). You can ask for coins, an item, or both.
- **Pays** - what *you* pay a shopper for bringing that item in.
- **Stock** - drop stacks into the stock slot to add stock, or press **Take** to pull it back out.

Every action saves immediately, so a disconnect never loses edits.

### One listing, two directions

A listing can go one way or both ways at once.

| You set | What the listing does |
| --- | --- |
| Price only | shoppers buy it from you |
| Pays only | shoppers sell it to you, you never sell it |
| Both | shoppers can do either |
| Neither | nothing. The editor tells you so |

Coins for **Pays** come out of the shop's **Earnings**, so leave money in the shop if you want to
buy things. If it runs dry the sale is refused.

### Restocking and limits

Below the Save row:

- **Restock** - refill on a clock: every game day, every game week, every real day, every real week,
  or **Never**.
- **Refill to** - the number stock is topped back up to when that clock ticks. It tops **up to** the
  number, it does not add to it, and it never cuts stock down.
- **Max each** - the most one player may **buy** between restocks. Their count resets on every
  restock. Leave it **off** for no limit.

### Prices that move

The **Fixed prices** button switches a listing between a set price and one that drifts with trade.

With drift on, buying pushes the price **up** and selling into the shop pushes it **down**. It never
drops below a quarter of your number or rises above four times it, and it drifts back to your number
on its own once trade stops.

### Buying From a Shop

Right-click someone else's shop NPC to open the **browse** screen:

- **Left-click a row to buy it.** Hold **Shift** to buy as many as a stack allows.
- **Right-click a row to sell it**, when the shop pays for that item. Hold **Shift** to sell 16.
- Each row reads left to right: what you hand over, an arrow, then what you get back.
- A red cross means you cannot buy that one right now. Stock updates live, and closed or rent-frozen
  shops cannot be traded with.

### Admin shops

Operators can press **Admin** in the Shop Manager to turn a shop into a server shop.

An admin shop has **endless stock** and **endless coins**, so it never sells out and never runs out
of money to pay people. Its rows show `inf` where a stock count would be. It does not collect
earnings either, since the coins it takes in are removed from the economy.

This replaces the old separate admin shop system. Every player shop feature works on one.

### Shop Rent (optional)

Admins can enable **shop rent** - a periodic coin cost per open shop (a sink). If the owner can't
pay, sales pause; after a grace period the shop auto-closes. See [Configuration](11.-Configuration).

### Shop Commands

| Command | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `/shop create <name>` | Create a shop (admin/aux; normally done via the NPC role) |
| `/shop list` | List your shops |
| `/shop open <id>` | Open a shop's screen |
| `/shop toggle <id>` | Open/close a shop |
| `/shop delete <id>` | Delete a shop (returns its contents) |
| `/shop relink <id>` | Put the NPC you are looking at behind a shop that lost its shopkeeper |

Admin shop commands are on the [Admin](Admin) page.

### If a shop's NPC goes missing

**Your shop and everything in it are still there.** A shop is saved separately from the NPC that
runs it, so losing the NPC never deletes the shop, its stock or its takings. It just leaves the shop
with nobody standing behind it.

To get it back:

1. `/shop list` and note the shop's short id
2. Place a new NPC and give it the **Shop** role
3. Look at that NPC and run `/shop relink <id>`

Placing a shop NPC will also tell you when you own a shop with nobody behind it, and print the
command for you.

> This is something you ask for rather than something that happens on its own, and on purpose. From
> the server's side an NPC standing in a chunk nobody has loaded looks exactly like a deleted one, so
> doing it automatically would sometimes take a shop off a shopkeeper that was only out of range.
> You know which of yours is gone.

Admins: `/shop admin cleanup` reports shops whose NPC could not be found anywhere, in any dimension.
It only reports. Actually cutting those links needs `/shop admin cleanup confirm`, and it cannot be
undone in game, so load the area first and check the list is really what you think it is.
