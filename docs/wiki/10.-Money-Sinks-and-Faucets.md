## Money Sinks & Faucets

A healthy economy needs coins to both **enter** (faucets) and **leave** (sinks). Notch Currency ships
a wide range of both, almost all configurable and off/tunable by default. Most are accessed through
an [Economy Role](09.-Economy-Roles) NPC or a placed block.

### Sinks (remove coins)

- **Enchanter** - pay coins to fully repair gear, buy a specific enchantment level, or extract an
  enchantment onto a book. (Enchanter role.)
- **Raffle** - buy tickets for a shared, growing pot. The house takes a configurable cut (the sink);
  the rest is redistributed to the winner. Tickets are physical items; there's a coins/item prize
  pool option for admins. (Raffle role.)
- **Gambling** - a **Slot Machine** and a **Coin Flip** block. Bet coins directly; a configurable
  house edge means the house nets a sink over time. (Also the Dealer role for slots.)
- **Crate keys** - keys are bought with coins to open loot crates.
- **Cosmetics shop** - buy cosmetics for coins. Datapack-driven, so it can sell any mod's cosmetic
  by giving an item or running an unlock command. (Cosmetics role.)
- **Wealth tax** - an optional periodic tax on balances above a threshold (only affects the wealthy).
- **Shop rent** - an optional periodic cost per open player shop.
- **Auction fees** - scaling listing fee + sale tax (see [Auction House](05.-Auction-House)).
- **Waystone fee** - an optional coin cost per Waystone teleport (needs the Waystones mod).

### Faucets (add coins)

- **Bounties** - a **Bounty Board** block (or Bounty role) posts auto-generating kill/fetch tasks
  with rarities and coin/item rewards. Take a bounty, complete it, and turn it in.

**Player bounties** are off by default. Turn on **Player bounties** in the config, and a **Post**
button appears on the Bounty Board. A player picks a monster to hunt from a list they can type to
search, or an item to collect, sets
a reward in coins, an item, or both, and posts it. The reward is held until the job is done, so
player bounties move money between players and never create it.

- One player at a time can take a player bounty. If they run out of time, it goes back on the
  board.
- Items handed in for a collect bounty go to the player who posted it, by mail if they are
  offline.
- The poster can take a bounty down while nobody is working on it, and gets the reward back.
- If nobody finishes it in time, the reward goes back to the poster.
- **Bounties a player can post** and **Player bounty lifetime (hours)** set the limits. Set
  **Live bounties on the board** to 0 to show only player bounties.

**PvP bounties** are off by default too, and need player bounties on. Turn on **PvP bounties**, and
the Post screen gets a third tab, **Player**, for a bounty on someone who is online. The hunter who
takes it and kills that player gets the reward. The target is told about it, and can't take the
bounty on their own head.
- **Loans** - borrow coins up to a cap and repay with interest over a term. Overdue loans incur a
  late fee and penalty interest. (Off by default - it creates money.)
- **Savings interest** - an optional periodic payout of a small percent of every balance, capped
  per payout so a huge balance cannot snowball. (Off by default - it creates money.)
- **World events** - balloon crates and golden caches (see [World Events](07.-World-Events)).
- **Boss loot** - bosses drop coins.

### The treasury

By default, money spent on a sink is gone for good. A server can turn on the **treasury** so that
spent money collects in a server bank instead, ready for admins to pay out. See
[Admin](Admin#treasury).

### Crates & Keys (detail)

- Crate types are **datapack-driven** (`data/<namespace>/crates/*.json`); the mod ships common, rare,
  and epic examples.
- Each crate needs a number of **keys** (bought with coins - a sink). Opening rolls weighted loot
  (items and/or coins - a faucet). Sneak-right-click a crate to see its odds.

### Cosmetics (detail)

- Offers come from data packs: `data/<namespace>/cosmetics/<name>.json`. Run `/reload` after a change.
- Admins see a **?** button on the Cosmetics shop. Hover it for the steps. Click it to copy an example file.
- A reward is an item or a command. In a command, `%player%` is the buyer's name and `%uuid%` is their UUID.
- With `"one_time": true`, each player can buy it once. After that the shop shows it as Owned.
- The mod ships two examples. To change one, put your own file at the same path, like
  `data/notchcurrency/cosmetics/example_hat.json`. To hide one, add `"enabled": false` to that file.

```json
{
  "name": "Party Hat",
  "icon": "minecraft:leather_helmet",
  "price": 250,
  "one_time": true,
  "reward": { "type": "item", "item": "minecraft:leather_helmet", "count": 1 }
}
```

A command reward looks like this:
`"reward": { "type": "command", "command": "give %player% minecraft:diamond 1" }`

### Loans (detail)

`/loan` opens the loan screen: borrow up to the configured cap, repay part or all, and watch your
due date. Interest compounds each cycle; auto-collect can pull spare balance toward the debt.

A debt cannot grow past the **debt ceiling**, 3x the borrowing limit by default, so a forgotten
loan stops at a number a player can still pay off. Set the ceiling to 0 to let it grow without
limit.

### Commands (quick list)

| Command | Feature |
| --- | --- |
| `/raffle`, `/raffle buy <n>`, `/raffle claim` | Raffle |
| `/slots`, `/coinflip heads\|tails <bet>` | Gambling |
| `/bounty`, `/bounty take\|claim\|turnin <id>` | Bounties |
| `/crate buykey [n]`, `/crate odds <type>` | Crates |
| `/loan`, `/loan borrow\|repay <amt>` | Loans |

Enchanter and Cosmetics are opened by right-clicking an NPC with that role.
