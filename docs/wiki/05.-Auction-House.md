## Auction House

A server-wide marketplace for buying and selling items. Open it from an **Auctioneer** NPC (see
[Economy Roles](09.-Economy-Roles)) or with `/ah`.

### Listing an Item

Click the **+ (list item)** button in the auction house, or run `/ah list`:

- Drop the item into the input slot.
- Set a **price**.
- Choose a **sale type**: **Buy Now** (instant sale at your price) or a **1 / 3 / 7-day auction**
  (highest bid after the timer wins).
- Confirm with **List It!**

### Buying & Bidding

- **Buy Now** listings are purchased instantly.
- **Timed auctions** take bids. When you bid, your coins are reserved; if someone outbids you, your
  reserve is **refunded** automatically (even if you're offline). Winnings and refunds are safe.

### Fees (optional, configurable)

Auction fees are money **sinks** and scale with the item's price:

- **Listing fee** - a flat amount **plus** a percent of the asking price, with an optional cap.
- **Sale tax** - a percent taken from the seller's payout, with an optional cap.

Both default to 0 (off). The **List an Item** screen shows the live fee as you type a price. See
[Configuration](11.-Configuration).

### My Listings & Cancelling

Open **My Listings** to see your active auctions. You can **cancel** a listing to reclaim the item;
if a timed auction already has a standing bid, cancelling **refunds the bidder** first.

### Mailbox

Auction winnings and unsold returns are held in a **mailbox** if you're offline - claim them from a
**Mailbox** NPC or when you next log in.

### Commands

| Command | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `/ah` | Open the auction house |
| `/ah list` | Open the "list an item" screen |
| `/ah list <price> [days]` | List your held item directly |
| `/ah cancel` | Show your listings with cancel buttons |
